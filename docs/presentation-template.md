# Official Presentation Template (markdown extraction)

> Source: `06_Official_Presentation_Template.pdf` — Interview Kickstart's material, **not
> redistributed here**; this file is a structural summary written for working against it.
> Slide-by-slide skeleton for the final deck (deck itself is a later-phase deliverable;
> capstone brief says ≤9 slides for the core narrative).

## Core slides (1–9)
1. **Title** — project/product name, brief description, course, capstone #, name, date.
2. **Problem & Context** — business context (1–2 lines); constraints & non-goals; what "good" looks like.
3. **Objectives & Scope** — what success looks like; in-scope / out-of-scope.
4. **Approach — System Overview** — high-level flow; key components/tools (n8n + APIs); design rationale (1–2 bullets).
5. **Data & Assumptions** — sample inputs (emails, catalog, inventory, CRM mock); key fields/entities extracted; top assumptions.
6. **Demo Scenarios & Flow** — 2–3 scenarios (happy path, edge); output snapshots; qualitative/proxy results.
7. **Limitations & Trade-offs** — what was simplified/omitted and why; known failure modes.
8. **Next Steps** — two immediate improvements; potential integrations/expansions; what to measure in a pilot (success metrics).
9. **Appendix / Links** — n8n JSON export, repo/data locations, references.

## Extended sections (optional, from full template)
- Problem Statement (WHAT / WHY / WHO-persona+scale)
- Research & Analysis (insights, competitive analysis, user research, visuals, citations)
- Solution (overview, key features, demo/prototype, competitive advantage)
- Implementation Strategy (roadmap, team/tech/budget/timeline, rollout plan, GTM)
- Business Model & Market Opportunity (revenue streams, cost structure, expected growth Y1/Y10)
- Vision & Metrics (north star, success, tracking, guardrail metrics)
- Challenges (potential risks, mitigation plan)
- Q&A, Appendix
